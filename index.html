01 — OUR STORY

ONE SCHOOL.
ONE JOURNEY.

In 2018, we entered Pinnawala Central College as Grade 6 students.

Years passed. We studied together, competed together, celebrated together — and eventually sat for the 2023 G.C.E. O/L examination as one batch.

Then our paths began to change. Some left school. Some continued their A/L journey. New places, new people and new experiences became part of our lives.

But one thing remains — we all came from the same place.
